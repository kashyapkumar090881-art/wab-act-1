Ticket & Stadium Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Keep your ticket ready for scanning at the entry gate. Each ticket is valid for one entry only.

ID Proof Requirement

Carry a valid government-issued photo ID that matches the details associated with your booking.

Prohibited Items

Do not bring outside food or drinks, weapons, fireworks, large bags, or other items prohibited by the venue.
